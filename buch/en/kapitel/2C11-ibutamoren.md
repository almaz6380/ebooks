## Ibutamoren (MK-677)

*Comparison chapter:* Ibutamoren is not a peptide. It is a small, synthetic molecule that is swallowed as a tablet or liquid – and that is precisely why it is in this book: as a point of comparison. It acts at the same receptor as ipamorelin, GHRP-2 or hexarelin, namely the ghrelin receptor, and raises growth hormone and IGF-1 for months. In the 1990s and 2000s, ibutamoren was tested by the pharmaceutical company Merck in several larger randomized trials. These trials show quite precisely what sustained stimulation of the growth hormone axis achieves in older people – and where the risks lie. On the gray market, ibutamoren is today usually sold as a "SARM-like supplement."

### Profile

- **Type and structure:** small synthetic molecule, not a peptide ("non-peptide agonist"; Liu 2021, PMID 34737341); orally active. Development names MK-677 and MK-0677, currently in development as LUM-201 (Bright 2022, PMID 35354138).
- **Origin:** developed by Merck (USA) in the 1990s as a "peptide mimetic" of the growth hormone–releasing peptides.
- **Mechanism of action:** agonist at the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a); amplifies existing GH pulses and raises IGF-1 persistently; increases appetite.
- **EU approval:** no. **US approval:** no.
- **WADA:** prohibited, class S2 (growth hormone secretagogues; ibutamoren is explicitly named).
- **Evidence level: B** – several randomized, placebo-controlled trials with up to 563 participants, but no approval and no trial with a positive primary clinical outcome.

### How it is supposed to work

Ibutamoren mimics ghrelin. Unlike the peptides in the group, it is not destroyed in the stomach and acts long enough to amplify growth hormone secretion around the clock with once-daily intake. In healthy older people, it nearly doubled the mean 24-hour concentration of growth hormone in one study; the number of GH pulses remained the same, but their height increased. After four weeks, IGF-1 reached levels like those of young adults (Chapman 1996, PMID 8954023).

Merck's hope was thereby to compensate for the age-related decline of the growth hormone axis known as "somatopause": muscle wasting (sarcopenia), frailty, bone loss and slow recovery after injuries.

### Human studies

**Scope of the evidence (search October 2026):** This book's automated search found **19** human studies in PubMed (`humanstudien_alle`) and **8** entries on ClinicalTrials.gov.

#### Healthy older adults

The most important trial ran for two years: 65 healthy women and men between 60 and 81 years of age received ibutamoren or placebo daily. Growth hormone and IGF-1 rose to the levels of young adults. Lean mass increased by 1.1 kg on ibutamoren and decreased by 0.5 kg on placebo. Abdominal fat did not change, body weight rose by 2.7 kg (placebo: 0.8 kg), and fat on the arms and legs increased more. The increase in lean mass did **not** lead to more strength or better function; the authors acknowledge that the trial was too small for that. Fasting glucose rose by an average of 0.3 mmol/L (5 mg/dL), and insulin sensitivity decreased. The most common side effects were initially increased appetite, mild fluid retention in the legs and muscle pain (Nass 2008, PMID 18981485).

#### Hip fracture

Two trials tested whether ibutamoren helps older people get back on their feet after a hip fracture:

- In the first trial, with 161 patients from 13 centers, IGF-1 rose by 84% (placebo: 17%). The functional tests showed some favorable trends but no statistically significant differences (Bach 2004, PMID 15066065).
- The second trial, with 123 patients, was **terminated early because heart failure occurred in a limited number of treated patients**. Gait speed had improved slightly, but most other functional measures had not; the authors judged the safety profile in this patient group to be unfavorable (Adunsky 2011, PMID 21067829).

Another Merck entry on sarcopenia after hip fracture (NCT00128115, 83 people) is listed as terminated, with no reasons or results given.

#### Alzheimer's disease

In a large trial, 563 people with mild to moderate Alzheimer's dementia received ibutamoren or placebo for one year. IGF-1 rose by 60.1% after six weeks and by 72.9% after twelve months. On none of the four scales for cognitive performance, activities of daily living and global impression did the course differ from placebo (Sevigny 2008, PMID 19015485).

#### Other studies

- **Overweight men:** In 24 men, lean mass increased over eight weeks, while total and abdominal fat did not change; blood glucose regulation worsened in the glucose tolerance test (Svensson 1998, PMID 9467542).
- **Osteoporosis:** In 292 postmenopausal women, ibutamoren together with the bone drug alendronate increased bone density more than alendronate alone only at the femoral neck, not at the spine or total hip (Murphy 2001, PMID 11238495).
- **Sleep:** In small studies with eight young and six older people, deep sleep and REM sleep, respectively, were prolonged (Copinschi 1997, PMID 9349662).
- **Dialysis:** In 22 dialysis patients, IGF-1 rose 65% more than with placebo, without serious side effects (Campbell 2018, PMID 28340044).
- **Children with growth hormone deficiency:** As early as 2001, ibutamoren increased growth hormone and IGF-1 in 18 children (Codner 2001, PMID 11452249). Under the name LUM-201, in a study with 68 children it triggered higher GH responses than the usual stimulation tests (Bright 2022, PMID 35354138).

### Animal and laboratory studies

In mice with an Alzheimer's model, ibutamoren reduced amyloid deposits and nerve cell loss in the brain (Jeong 2018, PMID 29912176) – a finding that did not translate into a clinical benefit in the large Alzheimer's trial in humans. The structure of the ghrelin receptor with bound ibutamoren was solved in 2021 (Liu 2021, PMID 34737341).

### Risks and side effects

**Established from randomized trials:**

- **Heart failure signal:** termination of a trial in older people after hip fracture because of cases of heart failure (Adunsky 2011, PMID 21067829). The abstract does not state how many cases there were. Fluid retention is among the known effects of elevated growth hormone levels and places a strain on a previously damaged heart.
- **Blood glucose:** rise in fasting glucose and reduced insulin sensitivity (Nass 2008, PMID 18981485; Chapman 1996, PMID 8954023); impaired glucose tolerance in overweight men (Svensson 1998, PMID 9467542).
- **Weight gain and appetite:** more body weight, more fat on the arms and legs, increased hunger (Nass 2008, PMID 18981485).
- **Fluid retention and muscle pain** (Nass 2008, PMID 18981485).

**Case reports from gray-market use:** A man in his early 30s developed elevated liver values after taking MK-677 for two months, which normalized after stopping (Cobani 2025, PMID 40675653). A 40-year-old developed breast growth and testosterone deficiency; his fitness supplements contained, in addition to MK-677, a SARM, cardarine and undeclared hormones (Chong 2024, PMID 39145153).

**Cancer:** Ibutamoren raises IGF-1 to the level of young adults for months to years. No cancer signal was reported in the trials, but they were neither large nor long enough for that. A review of GH releasers explicitly calls for long-term data on cancer and mortality (Sigalos 2018, PMID 28400207). For the general evidence, see the introduction to this group and Part IV.

**Gray-market quality:** In a US analysis of 44 products sold online as SARMs, 39% contained other unapproved substances instead of or in addition to the stated one, including ibutamoren; only 41% contained the stated amount of the active ingredient (Van Wagoner 2017, PMID 29183075). A European market surveillance study of 324 samples from 13 countries found ibutamoren to be the most common active ingredient among the muscle-building preparations examined; 24% of the products were presented as dietary supplements (Barrios 2025, PMID 40551438).

### Legal status

*As of 2026, please check for updates.*

- **EU, Germany, Austria:** no approval. Ibutamoren is an unapproved drug substance; it may not be marketed as a dietary supplement. Anyone offering it for use in humans violates medicines law.
- **USA:** no approval; nevertheless sold as a "research chemical" or in supplements.
- **WADA:** prohibited (S2). It is also controlled in equestrian sport, for example through hair analysis (Viljanto 2023, PMID 36354265).

### Ongoing studies and outlook

*Established:* Under the name LUM-201, ibutamoren is being developed as a tablet for children with growth hormone deficiency. A phase 2 trial with 104 children (NCT04614337) has been completed; a phase 3 trial with a planned 150 children has been recruiting since 2026 (NCT06948214), and a long-term safety study is planned (NCT07129759). Another phase 2 trial is to study LUM-201 from 2027 in addition to semaglutide in older people with obesity, in order to limit muscle loss during weight loss (NCT07754045).

*Author's assessment:* Approval for children is conceivable at the earliest after completion of the phase 3 trial, that is, not before the end of the 2020s. Approval for older adults is not in sight after the heart failure signal and the lack of functional improvements.

### Conclusion

Ibutamoren is the most thoroughly studied orally active agent that persistently stimulates the growth hormone axis. The trials show the basic pattern of the whole group: IGF-1 and lean mass rise, but strength, function and disease course – in aging, hip fracture and Alzheimer's – did not improve. Instead, blood glucose and weight rose, and one trial in older people was terminated because of heart failure. Gray-market products labeled "MK-677" are frequently mislabeled.
