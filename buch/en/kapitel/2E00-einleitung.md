# Part II · E – Hormones, Sexuality and Pigmentation

The six peptides in this group act on very different things: skin color, sexual desire, fertility and social behavior. What they have in common is that they mimic the body's own signaling pathways in the brain and the hormone glands, which are closely intertwined. Three of them belong to a single family, the melanocortin system; three others control the reproductive hormone axis or are connected to it.

### The Melanocortin System

It all starts with a large precursor protein called proopiomelanocortin (POMC). Enzymes cut several smaller peptides out of it, including the stress hormone ACTH and the melanocyte-stimulating hormones (MSH), above all alpha-MSH. Together, these messengers are called melanocortins. They act through five receptors, MC1R to MC5R, located in different tissues:

- **MC1R** on the pigment cells of the skin controls whether they produce dark, protective eumelanin. People with red hair and fair skin often carry less effective variants of this receptor.
- **MC2R** in the adrenal gland is the receptor for ACTH and regulates cortisol production.
- **MC3R and MC4R** in the brain influence appetite, energy expenditure and – as was discovered by chance – sexual arousal. Defects in MC4R are the most common genetic cause of severe obesity.
- **MC5R** acts on sebaceous and other glands, among other things.

The story of the three melanocortin peptides in this part begins at the University of Arizona. There, in the 1980s and 1990s, researchers developed two stabilized variants of alpha-MSH to produce a tan without sun: the linear melanotan I and the ring-shaped melanotan II (Hadley 2006, PMID 16412534). In trials with melanotan II, men reported spontaneous erections – the discovery that melanocortins in the brain influence sexual function (Hadley 2005, PMID 15996790).

Three paths emerged from these beginnings:

- **Melanotan I** was developed further under the name **afamelanotide** into an implant for a rare, painful sensitivity to light and is approved in the EU and the USA.
- **Melanotan II** was never approved but is widespread on the gray market as a "tanning injection."
- **Bremelanotide**, a variant of melanotan II, is approved in the USA for low sexual desire in women.

A fourth melanocortin drug, setmelanotide, specifically targets MC4R and is used in rare genetic forms of obesity; it is covered in Part II · A. The more selectively a drug addresses one receptor, the more targeted its effect. Melanotan II addresses almost all of them and therefore combines tanning, nausea, loss of appetite and erections in a single molecule.

### The Reproductive Hormone Axis

The other three peptides in this part act on the axis that controls puberty, the menstrual cycle and fertility. **Kisspeptin** switches on the GnRH nerve cells in the hypothalamus. **Gonadorelin** is the synthetic form of GnRH itself, which, released in pulses, stimulates the pituitary gland to secrete LH and FSH. Both illustrate a shared principle: given in the right rhythm, they activate the axis; as a continuous stimulus, they suppress it. Finally, **oxytocin** comes from the same brain regions, controls childbirth and breastfeeding, and has been studied for two decades as a nasal spray for social behavior – with sobering results.

### Overview

| Peptide | Evidence | Approval | In brief |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bremelanotide (PT-141) | A | USA (Vyleesi), not EU | Desire in women with HSDD; small effect, much nausea |
| Melanotan II | C | none | Gray-market tanning; case reports on moles and priapism |
| Afamelanotide | A | EU and USA (Scenesse) | Light protection in EPP; vitiligo under investigation |
| Kisspeptin | B | none | Egg maturation in IVF, hormone tests; research |
| Oxytocin (nasal spray) | B | only as infusion (obstetrics) | Autism and trust: large studies negative |
| Gonadorelin | B | national (EU), no longer in the USA | GnRH test and pump for infertility; banned in sport |

The approval information reflects the status as of 2026 and should be checked for updates.

### Reading Guide

If you are interested in tanning agents, read the chapters on melanotan II and afamelanotide together: they show how differently the same basic idea can turn out – once as a tested medicine in a specialized clinic, once as an untested powder from the internet. On the topic of sexuality, it is worth comparing bremelanotide, kisspeptin and oxytocin; all three show how strong placebo effects are in this field. Together, kisspeptin and gonadorelin explain the hormone axis of fertility. General information on gray-market products can be found in Part I, and a comparison of risks in Part IV.
