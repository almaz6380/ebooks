## Kisspeptin

### Profile

- **Type/structure:** a family of the body's own peptides. The KISS1 gene encodes a precursor protein from which pieces of different lengths are produced: kisspeptin-54 (54 amino acids, the predominant form in the blood), kisspeptin-14, -13 and -10. The shortest form, kisspeptin-10, already contains the fully active part. Studies use the natural forms as well as synthetic, more stable variants (such as MVT-602 and TAK-448).
- **Origin:** first discovered in 1996 as a gene that suppresses metastasis of melanoma cells (Lee 1996, PMID 8944003). The name goes back to the town of Hershey in Pennsylvania, home of the "Kisses" chocolate. In 2001, the peptide was identified as the messenger of a previously "orphan" receptor and initially named metastin (Ohtaki 2001, PMID 11385580).
- **Mechanism of action:** agonist at the kisspeptin receptor (KISS1R, formerly GPR54) on the GnRH nerve cells in the hypothalamus. Kisspeptin is the most important known trigger for the release of GnRH and thus for the entire reproductive hormone axis.
- **Approval EU:** no.
- **Approval USA:** no.
- **WADA:** not named explicitly. As a non-approved substance, kisspeptin falls under S0; in men, group S2 also covers luteinizing hormone releasing factors (as of 2026, please check for updates).
- **Evidence level:** **B** – numerous small, partly randomized studies and several randomized phase 2 trials, mainly from London and Boston. No phase 3 trial, no approval.

### How it is supposed to work

Human reproduction is controlled by a chain of hormones. At the top is the hormone GnRH (gonadotropin-releasing hormone) from the hypothalamus, which is released in pulses. It causes the pituitary gland to release the hormones LH (luteinizing hormone) and FSH (follicle-stimulating hormone). These in turn control the ovaries and testes: egg maturation and ovulation, sperm production, estrogen and testosterone production. The chapter on gonadorelin (2E06) describes this axis in more detail.

Kisspeptin sits one level above GnRH. Specialized nerve cells in the hypothalamus release it and thereby "switch on" the GnRH cells. How central this function is became clear in 2003: people with a defective kisspeptin receptor do not go through puberty and remain infertile (de Roux 2003, PMID 12944565; Seminara 2003, PMID 14573733).

Kisspeptin is of medical interest for two reasons:

1. **It acts "from the top."** Unlike medications that directly replace LH or FSH, kisspeptin stimulates the body's own GnRH release. The subsequent hormone release remains subject to the natural feedback mechanisms. This makes overstimulation of the ovaries less likely.
2. **Briefly it stimulates, continuously it suppresses.** With uninterrupted administration, the receptors become accustomed to the signal (tachyphylaxis), and the hormone axis shuts down. That is a problem if you want to promote fertility – and an opportunity if you want to lower hormones, for example in prostate cancer.

Kisspeptin receptors are also found in brain regions that process emotions and sexual stimuli. This gave rise to the idea that kisspeptin could influence sexual desire.

### Human studies

**Size of the evidence base (search October 2026):** The automated search for this book found **37** human studies in PubMed (`humanstudien_alle`) and **36** entries on ClinicalTrials.gov. Not all of them administer kisspeptin as a drug: some studies only measure kisspeptin levels in the blood, for example during pregnancy. The administration of kisspeptin in humans has been driven by a few research groups, mainly at Imperial College London and Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston. Most studies have fewer than 50 participants.

#### First Doses and Fundamentals

The first study in humans in 2005 involved six healthy men. A 90-minute infusion of kisspeptin-54 significantly increased LH, FSH and testosterone compared with saline (Dhillo 2005, PMID 16174713). The half-life in the blood was just under 28 minutes. In women, the effect depends strongly on the phase of the cycle and is strongest shortly before ovulation (Dhillo 2007, PMID 17635940).

In healthy men, kisspeptin-10 rapidly triggered a dose-dependent rise in LH and, with continuous infusion, increased the frequency of LH pulses (George 2011, PMID 21632807). Children with delayed puberty responded very differently: some had a clear response, others none (Chan 2018, PMID 29669934). In the future, this could help distinguish merely delayed puberty from a permanent hormone deficiency.

#### Fertility Treatment: Egg Maturation

The most advanced application to date is triggering egg maturation in in vitro fertilization (IVF). Normally, the hormone hCG is injected for this. hCG acts for a long time and in susceptible women can trigger ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS) – a fluid shift that can be life-threatening in extreme cases. Kisspeptin instead triggers a shorter LH surge from the body itself.

| Study | Participants and design | Duration | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jayasena 2014, PMID 25036713 | 53 women undergoing IVF, dose study without control group | one cycle | Egg maturation at all doses; clinical pregnancy 23% |
| Abbara 2015, PMID 26192876 | 60 women at high risk of OHSS, randomized dose study | one cycle | Egg maturation in 95%; live birth 45% per transfer; no moderate or severe OHSS |
| Abbara 2017, PMID 28854728 | 62 women at high risk of OHSS, RCT: second dose vs. saline | one cycle | Adequate egg yield in 71% vs. 45% |
| Abbara 2024, PMID 37925096 | 24 and 75 healthy women, two RCTs with the analog MVT-602 | single dose | LH rise similar to the natural mid-cycle; ovulation within 5 days in up to 100% |

In the first study (Jayasena 2014, PMID 25036713; NCT01667406), 53 women received a single kisspeptin injection. Eggs matured at all doses tested; embryos were transferred in 49 women, and twelve became clinically pregnant. This was the first demonstration that pregnancy is possible after kisspeptin-triggered egg maturation.

The follow-up study focused on women at high risk of overstimulation (Abbara 2015, PMID 26192876). None of the 60 women developed moderate or severe OHSS; the live birth rate per embryo transfer was 45%. A second kisspeptin dose ten hours after the first improved the yield of mature eggs (Abbara 2017, PMID 28854728).

**Context:** These studies show that the principle works. However, they were not designed to compare kisspeptin directly with established alternatives – in addition to hCG, triggering with a GnRH agonist is already available for high-risk patients. The authors themselves call for such comparative studies (Abbara 2017, PMID 28854728).

The synthetic analog MVT-602 acts considerably longer than natural kisspeptin: in healthy women, the LH peak came after around 21 instead of 5 hours, and the total amount of LH released was about four times as large (Abbara 2020, PMID 33196464). In two randomized studies with a total of 99 women, MVT-602 was well tolerated and, in the second study, triggered ovulation within five days in 75 to 100% of women depending on the dose – but also in 60% under placebo, because the participants were healthy (Abbara 2024, PMID 37925096).

#### Absent Menstruation and PCOS

In hypothalamic amenorrhea, menstruation stops because the brain – often due to underweight, intense exercise or stress – releases too little GnRH. A single kisspeptin injection strongly increased LH and FSH in affected women. With twice-daily administration over two weeks, however, the effect had almost disappeared (Jayasena 2009, PMID 19820030). Twice-weekly doses led to only partial desensitization (Jayasena 2010, PMID 20980998), and a continuous infusion temporarily tripled the number of LH pulses (Jayasena 2014, PMID 24517142). In people with a genetic defect in the neurokinin B system, a kisspeptin infusion restored pulsatile hormone release (Young 2013, PMID 22377698).

In polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), the situation is more complicated. In a pilot study with 12 women, kisspeptin increased the LH level only slightly; two women ovulated, while nothing happened in most (Romero-Ruiz 2019, PMID 31820802).

#### Men: Testosterone and Diabetes

In five men with type 2 diabetes and low testosterone, a kisspeptin-10 infusion increased LH, LH pulse frequency and testosterone (George 2013, PMID 23153270). A new study with 15 healthy men showed that daily eight-hour infusions under the skin maintained hormone release for twelve days, while a continuous infusion had no further effect on LH and FSH after five days (Yeung 2026, PMID 42549827). This is an important step toward a possible long-term treatment.

#### Sexual Desire and the Brain

The London group used functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) to study how kisspeptin changes the processing of erotic images.

| Study | Participants and design | Duration | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comninos 2017, PMID 28112678 | 29 healthy young men, crossover study vs. placebo | single infusion | More activity in limbic brain regions in response to sexual stimuli; less negative mood |
| Thurston 2022, PMID 36287566 | 32 women with HSDD, double-blind crossover study | 75 minutes | Altered activity in brain regions for sexual stimuli and attraction; no side effects |
| Mills 2023, PMID 36735255 | 32 men with HSDD, double-blind crossover study | 75 minutes | Altered brain activity; penile tumescence during erotic videos up to 56% greater than under placebo |

HSDD (hypoactive sexual desire disorder) means persistently low sexual desire with distress. In men with HSDD, a single kisspeptin infusion enhanced not only the activity of sexual brain regions but also measurable physical arousal and some reports of desire, such as "looking forward to sex" (Mills 2023, PMID 36735255). In women with HSDD, the changes in brain activity correlated with distress and sexual aversion (Thurston 2022, PMID 36287566).

**Context:** These studies test a single infusion in the laboratory, with brain activity as the primary endpoint. They say nothing about whether kisspeptin improves sexual experience in everyday life over weeks. Administration as an infusion is unsuitable for everyday treatment. A study with 95 people found no effect on anxiety, cortisol, blood pressure or heart rate (Mills 2025, PMID 40036336) – reassurance regarding tolerability, not proof of efficacy.

#### Nasal Spray

A nasal spray with kisspeptin-54 increased LH in healthy men and women and in women with hypothalamic amenorrhea, without side effects (Mills 2025, PMID 40215751). This points to a possible route to treatment without a needle. In mice, kisspeptin was detected in the olfactory mucosa, suggesting a pathway via the olfactory system.

#### Other Findings

- **Bone:** In 26 healthy men, the bone formation marker osteocalcin rose by up to 20.3% after kisspeptin administration (Comninos 2022, PMID 35244717).
- **Metabolism:** In 15 healthy men, kisspeptin increased insulin secretion after a glucose load without changing appetite or food intake (Izzi-Engbeaya 2018, PMID 29974637).
- **Oxytocin:** After a kisspeptin injection, the oxytocin level in the blood rose, more in men than in women (Galbiati 2025, PMID 39965102). This is being investigated as a possible test for oxytocin deficiency (chapter 2E05).

#### The Opposite: Lowering Hormones

With continuous administration, the synthetic analog TAK-448 became a hormone blocker. In healthy men over 50, testosterone fell to castration levels after one week of continuous infusion; in men with prostate cancer, a depot injection lowered testosterone and the tumor marker PSA (MacLean 2014, PMID 24762108). Two further phase 2 trials by the manufacturer were terminated early (NCT02369796, NCT02381288).

### Animal and laboratory studies

The role of kisspeptin in puberty and reproduction was first shown in mice with a knocked-out receptor gene; the animals remain sexually immature (Seminara 2003, PMID 14573733). Originally, the peptide was described as a brake on metastasis: in cell culture and in mice, it inhibited the spread of melanoma cells (Lee 1996, PMID 8944003; Ohtaki 2001, PMID 11385580). Whether this can be used therapeutically is open.

In rat models of PCOS, kisspeptin restored ovulation in some animals but not in others (Romero-Ruiz 2019, PMID 31820802). Basic research on kisspeptin and neurokinin B is summarized in a review (Koysombat 2025, PMID 39813600).

### Risks and side effects

#### Established

In all published studies, kisspeptin was well tolerated. Several studies explicitly report no side effects (Jayasena 2010, PMID 20980998; Thurston 2022, PMID 36287566; Mills 2025, PMID 40215751). Kisspeptin changed neither growth hormone nor prolactin nor the thyroid-stimulating TSH (Jayasena 2014, PMID 24863252) and had no influence on anxiety, blood pressure or heart rate (Mills 2025, PMID 40036336). With the analog TAK-448, mild to moderate side effects occurred in 26% of those treated (MacLean 2014, PMID 24762108).

#### Theoretical and Open Questions

- **Desensitization and hormone drop:** With continuous administration, the opposite of the desired effect can occur, as the studies on TAK-448 show. Anyone who uses kisspeptin without medical supervision therefore risks suppressing their own hormone axis.
- **Long-term data** are lacking. The longest studies ran for eight weeks.
- **Pregnancy:** Children have been born after use in IVF; systematic data on the health of these children have not been published.

#### Gray Market

Kisspeptin-10 is offered on the internet as a "research peptide" and promoted in forums for boosting testosterone or libido. There are no studies on such products. Because of the short half-life and desensitization with frequent administration, a lasting effect with self-administration is not established; purity and content are untested (see Part I and Part IV).

### Legal status

*As of 2026, please check for updates.*

- **EU, Germany, Austria:** no approval. Kisspeptin may only be used in humans within approved clinical trials. Sale for human use is not permitted.
- **USA:** no approval; use only in studies authorized by the FDA.
- **WADA:** In men, LH releasing factors are prohibited under S2, and non-approved substances in general under S0. Kisspeptin is not named explicitly, but in the author's assessment falls under these groups.

### Ongoing studies and outlook

**Established (registered studies):**

- **Massachusetts General Hospital, Boston:** several phase 1 and phase 2 trials with repeated kisspeptin administration under the skin in congenital hormone deficiency (idiopathic hypogonadotropic hypogonadism, NCT05896293, 36 people, until 2028) and in hypothalamic amenorrhea (NCT07224438, 20 people, until 2030). Another study uses kisspeptin as a test of GnRH nerve cell function (NCT07224490).
- **Imperial College London:** a study running since 2014 on hormone release with prolonged kisspeptin administration (NCT02081924, 76 people).
- A phase 2 trial with pulsatile kisspeptin administration in hypogonadism has been completed (NCT04648969); results had not been published in PubMed at the time of going to press.

**Author's assessment:** In terms of its fundamentals, kisspeptin is one of the most scientifically solid peptides in this book – its role in puberty is genetically proven. Clinical development is slow, however, because it is mainly carried by universities. Approval for egg maturation in women at risk of OHSS or as a diagnostic test for puberty disorders seems most realistic, but this would require larger comparative studies. For the treatment of low sexual desire, there is so far no evidence of any benefit in everyday life.

### Conclusion

Kisspeptin is the body's master switch for the reproductive hormones, as people with a receptor defect impressively demonstrate. In small studies, it reliably triggered LH release and enabled egg maturation without severe OHSS in women at high risk of overstimulation. Laboratory studies with brain imaging suggest effects on the processing of sexual stimuli but are no proof of benefit in everyday life. The greatest practical obstacle is desensitization with frequent administration, which can go as far as suppression of the hormone axis. Kisspeptin is not approved anywhere; outside of studies, there is no tested use.
