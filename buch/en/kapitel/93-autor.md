# About the Author

Josef Gallab has been deeply involved with peptides, sport and biohacking for more than five years. He is interested in what really advances health and performance – and what remains nothing more than a promise.

He knows the scene, the promises and the personal reports surrounding many of the peptides described in this book at first hand. That is precisely why it matters to him to keep enthusiasm and evidence clearly apart: to take the potential of peptides seriously without keeping quiet about the open questions.

With The Peptide Handbook, he wants to give readers the tool he himself lacked at the beginning – an honest overview of what the studies really show.
