## Englisch (EN)

**Title:** The Peptide Handbook
**Subtitle:** Every Human Study Compared – Effects, Risks, Legal Status and Outlook

**Description:**

Semaglutide, tirzepatide, BPC-157, Epitalon, collagen: few health topics are discussed as loudly as peptides. Between marketing claims, forum anecdotes and headlines, one thing is almost always missing – a sober look at the studies.

This handbook provides it. For 58 peptides, the PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov databases were systematically searched. More than 1,200 scientific sources are cited in the text, and the index in the appendix lists 3,216 publications of clinical studies in humans. Every source is given with its PMID or NCT number so you can check it yourself.

You will learn:
• what each peptide is, how it is supposed to work and what human studies actually show – with tables and an evidence level from A to D
• which side effects and risks are proven and which are only theoretical
• the legal situation in the EU, Austria, Germany and the USA, and what the anti-doping list says
• how the GLP-1 weight-loss drugs compare head to head
• why many "research peptides" from the gray market are a risk – with results from laboratory analyses
• which studies are under way and where research is heading until 2035

The 58 peptides range from approved medicines such as semaglutide, tirzepatide and liraglutide, to new agents such as retatrutide and CagriSema, to substances promoted online such as BPC-157, TB-500, CJC-1295, ipamorelin, Semax, Selank, Epitalon, MOTS-c and Melanotan II, as well as collagen peptides and cosmetic peptides.

One chapter sets typical marketing promises against the evidence and shows how to tell reliable claims from unreliable ones.

This book contains no dosages, no instructions for use and no sources of supply. It does not replace medical advice. It is written for everyone who wants to form their own fact-based judgment.
